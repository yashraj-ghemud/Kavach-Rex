01 / THE PREMISE

One model approving its own patch is not verification.
It’s the model agreeing with itself.

I

Hunt

A real mutation fuzzer hammers a C parser compiled with AddressSanitizer until it produces a genuine heap-buffer-overflow crash — then shrinks it to a 9-byte reproducer.

II

Patch

One council member writes a minimal unified diff, hard-constrained to the flagged function. Any diff touching anything else is rejected by code, not by prompt.

III

Prove

A different model family cross-examines the diff blind, then real execution decides: rebuild, re-run the crash, full regression corpus, 5× determinism replay.

02 / THE COUNCIL

Three models. Three checkable roles.

Proposer and judge sit on different model families — that split is the safety mechanism, not decoration.

RECON + TRIAGE

Scout

Qwen · Groq

First on scene. Turns raw ASan crash dumps into clean structured JSON — fast and cheap, because it runs on every crash the fuzzer finds, ranking them by risk.

PATCH AUTHORING

Smith

GPT-OSS · Groq

The craftsperson. Receives the vulnerable function plus Scout’s triage, returns a minimal unified diff. A code-level allow-list is the real firewall — out-of-scope diffs never reach review.

REVIEW + CERTIFICATION

Judge

Nemotron Ultra · OpenRouter

The reasoning-heavy seat. Plans the campaign, then reviews Smith’s diff without ever seeing Smith’s reasoning — fresh eyes from a different family — and issues CERTIFY or REJECT.

“If the same model proposes and approves its own patch, ‘verification’ is just the model agreeing with itself. Routing proposer and reviewer to different providers is the actual safety mechanism.”

05 / THE EVIDENCE

An audit trail you can actually read.

Every campaign ends with a flat JSON evidence ledger. Its heart is the council_calls array — a timestamped record proving the proposer and the reviewer were genuinely different models, not one model roleplaying both.

  • Every LLM call tagged with agent, model and role
  • Rejection rounds logged, not hidden
  • Reproducer, diff, regression and replay results attached
  • Residual risk stated in writing — honesty as a feature
